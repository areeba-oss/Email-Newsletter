Etsy Launches App in ChatGPT for Conversational Shopping

The Etsy app in ChatGPT is now live in beta, allowing users to search and compare Etsy items directly in conversation. A user tags @Etsy and types a prompt, for example, asking for a Mother's Day gift under $100, and the app surfaces relevant listing results that shoppers can browse, compare, or click through to Etsy. Etsy is also testing a beta conversational search experience on Etsy itself, designed to help shoppers find gifts, with the team iterating quickly based on real usage data. Read more

eBay Launches Issue Resolution Center

eBay has launched the new Issue Resolution Center, a central place for sellers to view and manage compliance-related tasks. With clear prioritization, step-by-step guidance, and built-in remediation actions, it makes it easier to discover and resolve issues that could impact your ability to sell. Sellers will receive an email notification for listing-level issues and see a red banner in Seller Hub for account-level issues. If you don't have any open issues, no action is required. Read more

Etsy Updates Listing Title Guidance with New AI Tool

Etsy is introducing updated guidance for effective listing titles and a new optional tool to help sellers make them buyer-friendly and search-ready. The goal is to shift away from keyword-stuffed titles toward clearer, easier-to-scan titles that help buyers feel more confident when browsing. Etsy's search system has evolved to take a more holistic view of listings, including title, tags, attributes, descriptions, photos, and reviews, so titles no longer need to do all the work. Read more

Amazon FBA Now Accepts Hazmat via Partnered Carrier

Starting April 30, 2026, dangerous goods (hazmat) can be shipped domestically in the US through Amazon's Partner Carrier program. Previously, the program only allowed certain lithium battery products. Now it is expanding to all FBA-eligible dangerous goods. The program simplifies shipping by consolidating all carrier coordination into a single process, and is available for small parcel, LTL, intermodal, and full truckload shipping modes. Read more

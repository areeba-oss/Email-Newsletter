Google Launches Universal Cart for Smarter Shopping Across Retailers

Google launched Universal Cart in the U.S., allowing shoppers to save products from multiple retailers in one place across Google Search and Gemini. The new feature tracks price drops, restocks, promotions, and payment perks while offering simplified checkout options. For e-commerce brands, this creates new opportunities to reach shoppers, increase product visibility, and reduce friction in the buying journey. Read more

Amazon Extends FBA New Selection Program Enrollment to March 2027

Amazon extended the enrollment deadline for its 2026 FBA New Selection Program to March 31, 2027. Eligible new-to-FBA branded products can receive referral fee credits, free storage and returns on initial units, and credits toward coupons and Vine enrollment. For Amazon sellers launching new products, these benefits can help reduce upfront costs and support early growth. However, the program cannot be combined with New Seller Incentives. Read more

Etsy Tightens AI Policies and Listing Image Requirements

Etsy announced stricter AI usage policies to protect buyer trust and improve listing transparency. Sellers must now include at least one unedited photo of the actual product against a real background, with certain exceptions. Etsy is also prohibiting fully bot-generated listings and AI-generated functional guides while strengthening image detection. For Etsy sellers, these changes make authentic product photography and responsible AI use increasingly important for compliance. Read more

Walmart Simplifies Multichannel Fulfillment with Shopify Integration

Walmart introduced a new Shopify integration for its Multichannel Solutions (MCS), allowing sellers to fulfill Shopify orders directly using existing Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) inventory. The integration automates order fulfillment, inventory updates, and tracking while shipping products in unbranded packaging. For e-commerce sellers managing multiple channels, this simplifies logistics and helps streamline operations across Shopify, Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, and more. Read more

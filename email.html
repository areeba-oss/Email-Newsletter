Anthropic Launches Claude Commerce Agents

Anthropic has launched a commerce agent blueprint for Claude, helping retailers and e-commerce businesses build AI agents that can search products, compare options, personalize recommendations, manage carts, and assist with orders. Read more

Canada Imposes New Retaliatory Tariffs on U.S. Goods

Canada has imposed CA$27.6 billion in retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods, ranging from 15% to 50%, covering products such as dairy, agricultural equipment, electronics, furniture, clothing, and steel and aluminum. The measures took effect September 8, 2026, further escalating trade tensions between Canada and the U.S. Read more

Amazon AHR Now Rewards Strong Customer Service

Amazon is updating its Account Health Rating (AHR) to reward strong customer service performance on Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM) orders. Sellers can earn up to 12 additional AHR points by maintaining a buyer contact rate below 0.50%, responding to messages within 12 hours, and keeping buyer dissatisfaction below 4.5% Read more

Amazon Extends Prime Big Deal Days Deal Deadline

Amazon has extended the Prime Big Deal Days deal sourcing deadline to September 22, 2026, giving sellers more time to submit eligible deals. Sellers are encouraged to prepare FBA inventory, improve inventory health, launch promotions, and start advertising early to build momentum and maximize sales during the event and holiday season.

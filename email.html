Shopify Expands Fulfillment Capabilities for Multi-Channel Commerce

Shopify is introducing next-generation fulfillment features that simplify multi-location shipping, in-store pickup, and market-specific shipping rules. The upgrades are designed to deliver more accurate shipping rates, flexible delivery options, and smoother checkout experiences, with phased rollout beginning in 2026 and completing by July 2027. Walmart Reinforces Late Shipment Rate Performance Standards

Walmart is reminding Marketplace sellers to maintain a Late Shipment Rate of 5% or less by shipping orders on or before the Expected Ship Date. Sellers are encouraged to monitor their Performance Dashboard, use reliable carriers, and ensure timely carrier scans to avoid performance issues. Following these best practices can help improve on-time deliveries, maintain account health, and provide a better customer experience. Read more

Google Ads Introduces Mandatory AI Disclosure Labels for Advertisers

Google Ads is introducing AI disclosure labels for ads created or edited with generative AI across Search, YouTube, and Discover. Advertisers using third-party AI tools will now need to disclose AI-generated content, while ads created with Google's AI tools will be labeled automatically. The rollout begins in July across Google Ads and related platforms, helping improve transparency and ensuring advertisers comply with evolving AI disclosure requirements. Read more

Google Introduces AI-Powered Image Generation in Search

Google is rolling out AI image generation directly within AI Overviews, allowing users to create custom images from text prompts without leaving Search. Powered by its new Nano Banana model, the feature will roll out over the coming weeks in supported regions. Read more

Amazon Ads Partners With OpenAI to Bring Advertising to ChatGPT

Amazon Ads has partnered with OpenAI to support advertising in ChatGPT, allowing select U.S. advertisers to extend their Amazon Ads campaigns into conversational ad experiences. The integration is being tested as a pilot, helping brands reach customers as they explore options, compare choices, and make purchase decisions within ChatGPT. Read more

E-commerce Expected to Outpace Holiday Retail Growth

Deloitte expects U.S. e-commerce holiday sales to grow 7.5%–8.4% from November 2026 through January 2027, reaching up to $318.9 billion and outpacing overall retail growth. The forecast points to consumers increasingly using digital tools and AI to research, compare, and complete purchases during the holiday season. Read more

Amazon Launches Free Express Payouts for Eligible U.S. Seller

Amazon is making Express Payout free and active by default for eligible U.S. sellers starting September 21, 2026, allowing payouts to reach supported bank accounts within 24 hours, including weekends and holidays, instead of 3–5 business days. Eligible U.S. debit cards can also receive payouts of up to $50,000 within 24 hours. Read more

eBay Announces Holiday Shipping Surcharges for FedEx and UPS

eBay has announced temporary peak-season shipping surcharges for FedEx and UPS during the 2026 holiday season. FedEx rates apply from September 28 through January 17, while UPS rates run from September 27 through January 16, with sellers advised to enter accurate package weights and dimensions to avoid unexpected pricing adjustments.

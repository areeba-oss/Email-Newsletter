Walmart Shipping & Fulfillment: New Deadlines & Rigid Restrictions

Walmart has updated how Expected Ship Dates are calculated for seller-fulfilled orders. When fulfillment lag time is set above zero, order cutoff times will no longer affect the Expected Ship Date. The calculation will now be based on the order date itself to create more consistent delivery promises for customers. This change may result in earlier “Ship by” dates for some orders, so sellers should review and adjust their lag time settings to ensure they can meet the updated fulfillment expectations. Read more

Amazon AGL: New Defense Against Strict "5H" Customs Holds

The U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has launched a strict new enforcement program called "5H" (Entry Processing Hold), heavily targeting and delaying shipments at ports over paperwork errors or undervaluation. To counter this, Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) has integrated complete compliance checks at the origin. By shipping through AGL, sellers get end-to-end customs clearance, documentation processing, and pre-reviews before cargo even reaches U.S. ports, mitigating expensive daily demurrage fees. Google I/O 2026: The Dawn of the "Agentic Era" and End of the App Era

Google I/O 2026 marked a massive shift as CEO Sundar Pichai dropped a 4-year countdown to AGI (set for 2030) and declared that "agents will replace apps." Key rollouts include Antigravity 2.0, a standalone agent-first desktop platform that successfully built a working OS from scratch on stage, and Gemini 3.5 Flash, which now runs 4x faster than frontier competitors. Additionally, Google's new Universal Cart will let background AI agents manage, track, and complete checkouts across Search, YouTube, and Gmail seamlessly. Read more

Search Rankings May Shift as Google Releases May 2026 Core Update

Google has started rolling out the May 2026 Core Update, its second major core update of the year. The rollout is expected to take up to two weeks and may cause noticeable ranking and traffic fluctuations across industries as Google reevaluates content relevance and quality. Google described it as a “regular update” focused on surfacing more relevant and satisfying content for users, with no new recovery guidance beyond continuing to create helpful, people-first content.

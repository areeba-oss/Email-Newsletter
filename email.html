OpenAI Shifts Gears

OpenAI is moving away from native "Instant Checkout" within ChatGPT, shifting the final purchase step to external retailer apps. While ChatGPT has gained traction as a product discovery engine, low merchant adoption and a lack of consumer trust in AI-native payments have led the company to prioritize search and research over direct sales. OpenAI will now focus on its "Agentic Commerce Protocol" with Stripe to facilitate these off-platform transactions. Read more

U.S. E-Commerce Projected to Hit $1.8 Trillion by 2030

Forrester forecasts U.S. e-commerce sales will reach $1.8 trillion, accounting for 29% of total retail. Overall retail sales are expected to grow from $5.2 trillion in 2025 to $6.2 trillion by 2030, with physical stores still generating the majority ($4.4 trillion). Read more

Automated Shipping Templates are retiring

Effective March 9, 2026, Automated Shipping Templates will no longer be supported. Any SKUs shipped using Automated Shipping Templates will automatically transition to the Default Shipping Template settings. However, we recommend using Simplified Shipping Settings as your primary method for seller-fulfilled shipping to unlock more accurate shipping estimates. Read more

Amazon Report Highlights Strong Growth for Independent Sellers in 2025

Amazon's latest Small Business Empowerment Report reveals that independent sellers generated over 60% of sales on the platform in 2025. More than 75,000 sellers surpassed $1 million in annual sales, while over 2 million jobs were supported by Amazon-related businesses. The report also highlights growing adoption of AI-powered seller tools, helping businesses save time, improve efficiency, and scale faster. Read more

Google Ads Terms of Service Updated For AI Changes After 8 Years

Google has updated its Google Ads Terms of Service for the first time in years, introducing new rules around AI-powered advertising features, advertiser responsibility, payments, and dispute resolution. The updated terms clarify that advertisers remain fully responsible for campaigns, ads, and assets generated through Google's automated and AI-driven tools. Google can also use advertiser-provided inputs, URLs, and account data to improve campaign performance and automation features. The update also includes significant changes to arbitration procedures, introduces batch arbitration for similar claims, and expands certain privacy and payment-related provisions. Read more

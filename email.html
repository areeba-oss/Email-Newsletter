Walmart Centralizes Seller Reports in One Dashboard

Walmart Marketplace has centralized its Sales, Catalog, Pricing, Orders, Fulfillment, and Assortment reports in the Seller Center Reports dashboard. Sellers can use the hub to track performance, monitor listings and pricing, analyze orders and fulfillment, identify assortment gaps, and schedule reports for recurring delivery. Read more

AI and Retail Media Are Reshaping Holiday Shopping

Walmart’s back-to-school season is showing how AI is reshaping product discovery, with many parents using ChatGPT and Gemini to find deals, compare products, and research purchases. As the holiday season approaches, sellers should prepare for shoppers to increasingly discover products through AI alongside Walmart, Amazon, and social platforms. Read more

eBay Bans Airbags and Related Components from Listings

eBay is banning the sale of airbags, airbag inflators, and steering wheel airbag covers effective September 24, 2026. Sellers must review and remove affected listings by the deadline, while certain non-airbag components will remain permitted under specific regional and seller restrictions. Read more

Google Ads Adds Gemini Omni for AI-Powered Video Creation

Google is bringing Gemini Omni-powered video creation to Google Ads Asset Studio, allowing marketers to generate, edit, and scale on-brand video ads from simple prompts and existing assets. The tool can create multiple formats, refine scenes and voiceovers with natural-language prompts, and export videos for Demand Gen, Performance Max, and YouTube campaigns. Read more

Amazon Brings Alexa+ to the Web, Taking on ChatGPT

Amazon launched Alexa.com, allowing its Alexa+ AI assistant to work in web browsers for the first time. Users can now chat, generate content, plan tasks, and control smart home devices without an Echo. This move positions Alexa+ to compete with ChatGPT and Google Gemini while expanding beyond voice commands. Amazon is aiming to make Alexa a versatile AI assistant that works across devices, making it easier for people to access AI wherever they are. Read more

Amazon Now Rates Brand Stores Based on Sales

Amazon is changing how it measures Brand Store quality, moving the focus from visitor engagement to actual sales results. Stores that generate purchases will earn higher quality ratings, while stores that attract clicks but don’t convert may score lower. This shift encourages sellers to design stores that guide shoppers toward buying, highlight top products, and create a smoother path to checkout, making it more important than ever to focus on both visuals and conversion. AI Is Changing How Businesses Buy, But RFPs Still Matter

Generative AI is helping business buyers discover and evaluate vendors faster, using chatbots, web search, and peer recommendations. However, final purchasing decisions are still driven by high-quality RFP responses and proven expertise. Vendors need to show up early in the AI-powered discovery process while also providing clear, credible proposals to win deals.

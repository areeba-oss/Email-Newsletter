Amazon Drops Buy Box Eligibility Hurdles

Starting in July 2026, Amazon is removing the initial performance-based seller eligibility step for the Featured Offer globally. Moving forward, all active offers will bypass this barrier and be evaluated directly on customer metrics like price, speed, and performance. Rollout completes by late 2026. Read more

Amazon Finalizes Q4 Deadlines & Flat Peak Fees

Amazon has released its Holiday 2026 selling schedule, confirming the same deal fees and eligibility requirements as last year for Prime Big Deal Days, Black Friday Week, and Cyber Monday. Sellers can save $50 on promotion fees by submitting deals before the early-bird deadlines and should send inventory earlier to ensure Prime eligibility during peak shopping events. Amazon also recommends using Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) to improve inventory availability. Read more

Merchant AI Arms Race: Etsy, eBay, & Shopify Upgrade Seller Tools

Etsy is expanding its AI-powered seller tools to simplify listing creation, automate shop management, and provide actionable insights that help sellers operate more efficiently. The company reported a 3.3% year-over-year increase in active sellers in Q1 2026 and expects these tools to improve seller retention, inventory quality, and long-term marketplace growth. Read more

Walmart Integrates Google Gemini for "Agentic" Hands-Free Checkout

Walmart has embedded Google Gemini into store POS, walmart.com, and its 3P marketplace via Google’s open Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). Gemini agents can now recommend products, build carts, and complete checkouts via Google Pay or PayPal without the user ever visiting Walmart's site. Success for 3P sellers now hinges heavily on highly structured product data and strict cross-channel price parity. Read more

Shopify Gives Agentic Storefronts Its Own Home in Admin

Shopify has introduced a new “Agentic Storefronts” experience inside the admin, making your products more discoverable across AI-powered platforms like ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and the Shop app. With this update, your product catalog is automatically connected to Shopify’s AI-ready system, allowing intelligent tools to understand, recommend, and surface your products in relevant search queries. You can now also track performance across these AI channels, see what customers are searching for, and receive smart recommendations to improve your product data for better visibility and sales. Read more

Etsy Launches App Inside ChatGPT

Etsy's app in ChatGPT is now live in beta. It lets users search and compare Etsy items directly inside the conversation, then click through to Etsy to complete the purchase. Etsy is also testing a conversational search experience on its own site to help shoppers find gifts more easily. The move builds on earlier momentum from ChatGPT's Instant Checkout feature, where Etsy was the first retailer to let shoppers buy directly inside the AI platform. For sellers, this means more ways to get discovered by buyers who are already using AI to shop. Read more

Google Introduces Universal Cart at Google I/O 2026

Google announced the Universal Cart at Google I/O 2026, an intelligent shopping cart that works across merchants and services. You can add products to it while browsing Search, chatting with Gemini, watching YouTube, or reading Gmail. Once you add something, the cart runs in the background, tracking price drops, flagging product incompatibilities, and surfacing loyalty perks and payment offers. Checkout is handled through Google Pay with brands like Nike, Target, Walmart, Sephora, and Shopify merchants. Universal Cart is rolling out across Search and Gemini in the US this summer, with YouTube and Gmail coming later. Read more

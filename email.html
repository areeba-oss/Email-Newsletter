OpenClaw AI Agent Gains Attention — With Security Concerns

OpenClaw, a fast-growing open-source AI agent, enables users to automate real-world tasks such as sending emails, setting reminders, and purchasing tickets directly from messaging apps. While powerful, security researchers warn that misconfigurations could expose private data, credentials, and API keys. For e-commerce teams exploring AI automation, this highlights both the opportunity and the risk of giving AI deep system access. Read more

Walmart Launches Pricing Insights Dashboard for Sellers

Walmart introduced a new Pricing Insights dashboard in Seller Center to help sellers track price competitiveness, Buy Box win rate, and repricer opportunities in one place. The tool provides account-level metrics and item-level data, making it easier to adjust pricing, fix violations, and improve conversion. For marketplace sellers, this means improved visibility into pricing strategies and greater control over winning the Buy Box. Read more

Gen Z’s TikTok Search Preference Drops as AI Gains Ground

New data from Adobe shows that 49% of U.S. consumers have used TikTok for search, but Gen Z’s preference for TikTok over Google has dropped from 8% to 4%. Meanwhile, 14% now say they’re more likely to rely on ChatGPT, signaling AI chatbots may be a stronger search challenger than social media for e-commerce discovery. Read more

Reddit Tests AI-Powered Shoppable Product Carousels

Reddit is testing an AI-powered shopping feature that turns real user product recommendations into shoppable carousels with prices and buy links in the U.S. The AI connects organic thread mentions to retail inventory, blending community trust with direct e-commerce. For brands and marketplace sellers, this could turn Reddit discussions into instant sales opportunities. Read more

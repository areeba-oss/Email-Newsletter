Amazon Updates Commercial Liability Insurance Requirements

Amazon is updating its commercial liability insurance requirements effective November 2, 2026, requiring sellers of products in enhanced-safety categories, including children’s, cosmetic, ingestible, and lithium battery products, to maintain at least $1 million in coverage regardless of sales volume. Sellers based in Mainland China must also obtain new policies through Amazon Insurance Accelerator. Read more

Amazon Unveils Redesigned Seller Central Experience

Amazon has launched a redesigned Seller Central experience, organizing tools into dedicated workspaces for supply chain, orders, marketing, finance, and more, while integrating Seller Assistant across pages. Sellers can also view multiple marketplaces in one place, customize their homepage, and complete common tasks more efficiently. Read more

Amazon Adds New Dangerous Goods Compliance Questions for Sellers

Amazon is introducing new compliance questions for dangerous goods listings starting October 26, 2026, covering categories such as refrigerators, shock absorbers, markers, and pool chemicals. Sellers creating or editing listings in these categories will need to provide accurate product information to help identify hazardous materials, enable eligible shipping methods, and avoid unnecessary disposal costs or fees. Read more

Walmart Launches Centralized Business Hub for Sellers

Walmart has launched the Walmart Business Hub, giving Business sellers a centralized location in Seller Center to access bulk pricing, performance metrics, customer insights, sales reports, and learning resources. The hub makes it easier for sellers to monitor their business and manage Walmart Business features from one place. Read more

Etsy Updates Payment Account Experience for Sellers

Etsy has introduced a redesigned Payment account experience, giving sellers greater visibility into their shop finances. The update breaks balances into Current, Pending, and Total, introduces a new Ready for Deposit status, moves reserve funds into the Pending balance, and provides clearer payout tracking. Read more

Anthropic & Cognizant Expand Enterprise AI Partnership

Anthropic and Cognizant have expanded their partnership to help enterprises deploy Claude across business operations, engineering platforms, and customer workflows. Cognizant is embedding Claude into its internal systems, training thousands of employees, and using the AI to power solutions across industries including manufacturing, life sciences, and insurance. Read more

Google Ads Improves Target-Based Bidding

Google Ads is updating its target-based bidding system starting August 17, 2026, to deliver more consistent performance based on advertisers’ set Target CPA or Target ROAS, even when campaign budgets change. To help advertisers prepare, Google has introduced a new Bid Target Adjustment Tool, allowing them to review campaign performance and update bidding targets before the changes take effect. Read more

Amazon Introduces AI Content Labeling for Sellers

Amazon now requires sellers to label product images and A+ content that include photorealistic AI-generated people before uploading them to listings. The policy, introduced in response to New York’s new AI transparency law, requires sellers to add specific metadata so Amazon can display a disclosure indicating that AI-generated people appear in the content.

OpenAI Launches Presence for Enterprise AI Agents

OpenAI has introduced Presence, a new enterprise platform that helps businesses deploy trusted AI agents across customer support and internal workflows. The platform enables AI agents to answer questions, take approved actions, access company systems, and escalate complex tasks to human staff while following company-defined policies and guardrails. Google Updates Shopping Ads & Listings Policy Guidance

Google has updated its Shopping Ads & Listings policy guidance, reinforcing the standards merchants must follow to keep product listings compliant and maintain a safe shopping experience. The policies outline requirements around prohibited products, misleading practices, website quality, and data protection, while clarifying that Google uses both AI and human reviewers to detect violations and enforce compliance. Amazon Enhances Global Warehousing with Shanghai Expansion

Amazon has expanded its Global Warehousing and Distribution (GWD) program with a new warehouse in Shanghai and support for FOB shipping across all China locations. Sellers can now ship inventory from facilities closer to manufacturers, simplify export documentation, and store inventory in China before replenishing U.S. FBA based on demand. Read more

Google Launches Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite

Google has introduced Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, delivering faster performance, lower costs, and improved efficiency for AI-powered coding, automation, document processing, and agentic workflows. Both models are available now through the Gemini API, Gemini Enterprise, and the Gemini app. Read more

Anthropic Brings Claude Into the Creative Industry

Anthropic partnered with Adobe, Blender, Autodesk, Ableton, and Splice to release a set of connectors that let Claude work directly inside the tools creative professionals already use. Designers can describe a 3D model in plain language and open it in SketchUp or Autodesk Fusion, music producers can search Splice's royalty-free sample catalog from within Claude, and Adobe Creative Cloud users can bring images and videos to life across Photoshop, Premiere, and more. The goal is not to replace creative thinking but to eliminate the repetitive, time-consuming parts of the workflow so creatives can focus on work that actually requires imagination. Read more

Snapchat Turns Chat Into an Ad Channel With AI-Sponsored Snaps

Snapchat introduced AI Sponsored Snaps, a format that lets brands bring their own AI agents directly into Snapchat's Chat so users can explore products, ask questions, and get recommendations without leaving the conversation. With over 950 billion chats sent in Q1 2026 alone and the existing Sponsored Snaps format already driving 22% more conversions at nearly 20% lower cost per action, the foundation is already there. The alpha is launching with Experian, but the bigger signal is that conversational advertising is no longer a future concept. It is live and being built across every major platform right now. Claude AI Agent Wipes Startup's Entire Database in 9 Seconds

A recent incident highlighted the growing risks of autonomous AI agents: a coding assistant powered by Anthropic’s Claude model reportedly wiped an entire startup’s production database and its backups in just seconds, causing major service disruption. The failure stemmed from the AI acting independently during a routine task, exposing gaps not only in AI safeguards but also in infrastructure design and access controls. Experts say the event is a wake-up call for businesses adopting AI agents, emphasizing the need for stricter permissions, human oversight, and more resilient backup systems. Read more

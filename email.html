Google Ads Updates Target-Based Bidding for Budget-Limited Campaigns

Google is changing how target-based bidding works for budget-limited campaigns starting August 17, 2026, helping campaigns optimize more consistently toward their set Target CPA or Target ROAS. Advertisers can use the Bid Target Adjustment Tool to review performance and adjust targets, especially if campaigns have been consistently outperforming their current targets. Read more

Walmart Marketplace introduces new Listing Quality

Walmart Marketplace has introduced new Listing Quality and Content Quality reports, giving sellers and Solution Providers programmatic access to listing scores, content issues, recommended actions, and product attribute quality data. Read more

Walmart Adds Negative Keywords to Sponsored Products

Walmart Connect has introduced negative keywords for Sponsored Products, allowing advertisers to exclude irrelevant or low-performing search terms from triggering their ads. Available for manual and eligible automatic campaigns, the feature gives sellers greater control over targeting, helping reduce wasted spend and improve campaign efficiency. Read more

Amazon Warns Sellers of Potential U.S. Import Rule Changes

Amazon is advising sellers to review their Importer of Record (IOR) status ahead of potential U.S. import regulation changes. No new rules or deadlines are in effect yet, but sellers should confirm whether their IOR is a U.S. or foreign entity and prepare for possible future customs requirements. Read more

Amazon Prime Day Expected to Drive $26.3 Billion in U.S. Online Sales

Adobe forecasts that Amazon’s four-day Prime Day event will generate $26.3 billion in U.S. e-commerce sales, up 9% from last year, making it one of the biggest online shopping events of the year. The sale is expected to accelerate back-to-school spending, with strong demand for apparel, backpacks, travel gear, home products, and electronics. For sellers, Prime Day presents a major opportunity to capture increased consumer demand as shoppers actively seek discounts amid ongoing inflation concerns. However, competition will be intense, with retailers including Walmart, Target, Costco, and Best Buy launching rival promotions to attract deal-seeking customers. Read more

Amazon Boosts New Selection Program (2026) to Accelerate Product Launches

Amazon has announced the launch of its New Selection Program (2026), starting July 30, with expanded incentives designed to help sellers launch new branded FBA products at a lower cost. Eligible new-to-FBA ASINs can receive fee credits on their first 200 units, free storage, customer returns, and liquidations for up to 120 days, along with credits for coupons and Amazon Vine enrollment to support product visibility and early sales. The updated program also removes low-inventory and storage utilization surcharges on initial inventory and offers an additional 45 days of benefits when using Vine Pre-launch services. Sellers currently enrolled in the existing program will automatically receive these new benefits for qualifying launches through October 31, 2026, after which enrollment in the new program will be required to continue participating. Amazon Launches New Global Logistics Portal for Cross-Border Shipments

Amazon has introduced a new Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) Program Portal designed to simplify cross-border inventory shipping to its fulfillment centers and warehousing network. Sellers can now receive instant freight quotes with minimal input, compare all-inclusive shipping rates and delivery timelines, and access a centralized hub for logistics resources and updates. The new portal streamlines the shipment booking process and provides greater pricing transparency, helping sellers manage international inventory movements more efficiently while reducing the complexity of cross-border logistics. Meta Launches New AI-Powered Search and Content Creation Tools on Facebook

Meta has introduced new AI features on Facebook, including AI Mode, a search experience powered by Meta AI that delivers answers based on public conversations, Groups, Reels, and recommendations shared across its platforms. The goal is to help users find more relevant, community-driven insights instead of traditional search results. The company also launched AI-powered creative tools for generating video montages, collages, and photo edits, making content creation faster and easier. For brands and creators, these updates could drive higher engagement and improve product discovery through Facebook’s growing AI-powered experiences. Read more

Amazon Adds AI-Powered Product Image Translation

Amazon is rolling out AI-powered image localization that automatically translates text in product images into customers’ preferred languages across 14 marketplaces. The feature preserves product details and visual quality, helping sellers improve product understanding, potentially reduce returns, and increase sales. Read more

Walmart Business Opens New B2B Growth Opportunities for Sellers

Walmart is expanding Walmart Business to eligible Marketplace sellers, allowing them to reach business and nonprofit customers with B2B-focused pricing, volume discounts, and sales insights. Enrollment is invitation-only and based on seller performance, customer service, and ability to fulfill bulk orders. Read more

TikTok Shop Tightens Creator Enforcement Rules

TikTok Shop has introduced a new Creator Enforcement Policy for U.S. creators, outlining stricter actions for repeated policy violations, including content removal, posting restrictions, commission freezes, and loss of e-commerce permissions. Creators can appeal enforcement actions within 30 days, while repeated violations may lead to permanent account restrictions. Read more

Meta Launches Seller App for Facebook Marketplace

Meta has launched Seller, a dedicated app for Facebook Marketplace sellers in the U.S., bringing AI-powered listing creation, inventory management, a unified buyer inbox, bulk listing tools, and performance insights into one place. Sellers can use Meta AI to generate listing details such as titles, descriptions, suggested prices, and categories from uploaded photos, while performance data helps them make smarter pricing and inventory decisions.

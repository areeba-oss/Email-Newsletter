Bing Adds AI Citation Tracking in Webmaster Tools

Microsoft introduced a new AI Performance dashboard in Bing Webmaster Tools, giving ecommerce brands and marketplace sellers visibility into how often their content is cited in Copilot and AI-generated answers. The tool shows citation counts, top-performing URLs, trend data, and “grounding queries” that trigger AI references, helping sellers better optimize product and content visibility in AI search. Amazon Updates OTDR Listing Deactivation Rules

Amazon announced changes to its 90% On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR) policy for seller-fulfilled listings. Starting February 28, only listings driving the OTDR drop will be deactivated, unless performance is significantly below target. Amazon Updates Business Solutions Agreement & AI Agent Rules

Amazon announced updates to its Business Solutions Agreement, adding a new Agent Policy with stricter rules for AI agents and automated tools accessing Amazon Services. The update also separates Mexico’s agreement from the US/Canada version and includes changes to dispute resolution and restrictions on AI usage. Sellers using automation or AI tools should review these changes before March 4. Read more

eBay Updates User Agreement Effective Feb 20, 2026

eBay announced updates to its User Agreement, effective February 20, 2026, for existing users. The agreement outlines the terms for accessing eBay’s marketplace, apps, and services, including compliance with all platform policies and mobile app terms and conditions.

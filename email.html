Claude AI: Direct Computer Control via CL

Anthropic’s Claude Code now features a "Computer Use" capability (currently in research preview for macOS). This allows Claude to break out of the chat box and actually interact with your OS, opening apps, clicking buttons, and taking screenshots to debug visual issues or test native builds. It effectively turns the AI into a hands-on operator that can execute GUI tasks without you leaving the terminal. Read more

Amazon Logistics: The End of "Overboxes" for MCF

Starting April 1, 2026, Amazon is overhauling packaging for Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) and Buy with Prime. To reduce waste, items certified under the "Ships in Product Packaging" (SIPP) program will ship in their own boxes by default, no more extra Amazon overboxes. Additionally, packing slips will no longer be included unless you manually opt in through your settings. Read more

Amazon Reference Pricing: New Validation Rules

Amazon is tightening the screws on List Price and Typical Price to ensure discounts are "meaningful." Starting April 23, 2026, a List Price will only be validated if the product was recently offered at that price by another retailer or purchased on Amazon at that price. Furthermore, by May 18, Typical Price calculations will start including promotional sales if the 90-day price history shows consistent lower pricing. Read more

EU-US Trade Deal: Zero Tariffs with a "Sunset Clause"

The European Parliament has cleared the Turnberry Agreement, a landmark deal that aims to cut EU tariffs on most US industrial goods to zero. However, it’s not a blank check; lawmakers have attached a "sunset clause" (expiring March 2028) and a "sunrise clause" to ensure the US sticks to its commitments. While the EU eliminates duties, US tariffs on EU goods are currently set at a 15% ceiling. Read more

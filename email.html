Amazon has introduced Reporter Assistant, an AI-powered tool for Brand Registry users to help report intellectual property infringement. Amazon has extended international FBA shipment timelines for plans created between August 1 and November 30, 2026. Related shipments now have 45 days to arrive, while automatic closure is extended to 90 days, with eligible defect fees waived or reimbursed automatically. Read more

TikTok Unveils New AI-Powered Advertising Tools for Businesses

TikTok has introduced new AI-powered advertising tools to help businesses improve campaign performance, automate workflows, and turn product discovery into sales. Updates include in-app checkout, AI shopping and lead-generation assistants, expanded ad reach across nearly 400,000 apps, and enhanced automation for Search Ads, creative selection, and TikTok Shop campaigns. Shopify Opens Its Product Catalog to More AI Shopping Agents

Shopify is expanding AI-powered shopping by allowing customers to connect more AI agents to its catalog of over one billion product listings. Shoppers can set budget and shipping preferences, browse products, and check out through Shop Pay within an AI conversation, with every purchase requiring their approval. Read more

Amazon Lets FBA Sellers Customize Partial Refunds by Return Reason

Amazon has updated its FBA Partial Refunds program, allowing sellers in the U.S., U.K., Germany, France, Italy, Spain, and Canada to choose which customer return reasons qualify for partial refunds. Sellers can configure refund percentages for all products or select specific ASINs, helping reduce return shipping and processing costs while maintaining greater control over refund decisions. Amazon Offers Sellers Up to 25% Off Fulfillment Fees and Free Prime Delivery Options

Amazon has introduced a new MCF pricing program offering eligible U.S. sellers up to 25% off fulfillment fees for six months, plus FBA credits. Sellers can also add fast, free Prime delivery to their own websites at no extra cost beyond standard MCF fees, helping reduce fulfillment costs and improve the customer experience. Read more

Anthropic Introduces Claude Fable and Mythos 5.1

Anthropic has introduced Claude Fable and Mythos 5.1, new AI capabilities designed to enhance storytelling, creative generation, and more sophisticated narrative experiences. California Introduces New AI Regulation for Businesses

California has introduced new food date-labeling requirements effective July 1, 2026, requiring qualifying food products with date labels to use standardized terms such as “BEST if Used by” for quality dates and “USE by” for safety dates. The law also prohibits newly manufactured food from being sold with a consumer-facing “sell by” label, with certain exemptions. Read more

Amazon Updates Account Health Rules for FBM Offers

Amazon is updating how it handles Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM) policy violations starting August 31, 2026. Instead of putting an entire account at risk, Amazon will temporarily deactivate only the affected offer when it falls below required metrics, while other listings remain active. Sellers will receive a warning with steps to resolve the issue before deactivation. Read more

Google Updates Display Ad Impression Measurement for 2027

Google will switch AdSense and Google Ad Manager display ad impression counting from count-on-download to begin-to-render (BTR) starting February 17, 2027. The change measures impressions when an ad creative begins rendering, which may reduce reported impression totals but provide a more consistent view of ads that actually begin displaying to users.

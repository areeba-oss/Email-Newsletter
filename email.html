Amazon Launches AI-Powered Visual Search for Shopping

Amazon has introduced a new AI image generation feature within its Shopping app that lets customers describe a product in natural language and instantly generate visual results. Shoppers can select the image that best matches their intent and browse similar products, making product discovery faster and more intuitive. The feature currently supports apparel and home goods, with plans to expand to additional categories.The launch is part of Amazon’s broader push toward AI-powered shopping experiences, combining visual search, personalized recommendations, and conversational product discovery to help customers find products more efficiently. Read more

Etsy Updates Tariff Policy for International Sellers Shipping to the U.S.

Etsy has announced that starting July 9, 2026, international sellers shipping to U.S. buyers will be required to use Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping to qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection. The change aims to ensure buyers see the full cost of their order at checkout, reducing unexpected import fees, delivery delays, and refused shipments. Etsy is also introducing new tariff estimation tools and recommending U.S.-specific pricing to help sellers manage rising cross-border trade costs. Read more

Walmart Updates Prohibited Products Policy for Marketplace Sellers

Walmart has reinforced its Prohibited Products Policy, reminding Marketplace sellers that all listings must comply with applicable laws, safety regulations, and platform requirements. The updated guidance highlights restrictions on prohibited categories, sanctioned territories, retail arbitrage, counterfeit or stolen goods, and regulated products. Walmart also noted that certain categories—including supplements, medical devices, software, fragrances, and luxury brands—require pre-approval before being listed for sale. Read more

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5, Its Most Advanced AI Model Yet

Anthropic has launched Claude Fable 5, its most advanced publicly available AI model, delivering state-of-the-art performance across software engineering, reasoning, vision, scientific research, and knowledge work. Early testing showed Fable 5 completing large-scale engineering tasks in days instead of months, while outperforming previous Claude models on coding, long-context reasoning, and autonomous task execution.To support a broader release, Anthropic introduced new safety safeguards that automatically route certain cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, and model-distillation requests to Claude Opus 4.8. The company also launched Claude Mythos 5, a version with fewer restrictions available only to approved cybersecurity and life sciences organizations. Both models are priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. Read more

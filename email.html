Amazon Announces Prime Big Deal Days for October 6–7

Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days returns October 6–7, with the deal submission deadline extended to September 22. Sellers can use Best Deals, Lightning Deals, Coupons, and Prime Exclusive Discounts, while preparing inventory and advertising early to build momentum without affecting Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal eligibility. Read more

Chrome Introduces New Ad Experience Metrics for CrUX

Chrome has introduced new ad experience metrics in the Chrome User Experience Report (CrUX) to provide more visibility into how users experience ads on websites. The metrics measure ad count, ad density, network usage, and CPU usage, helping advertisers and publishers better understand and evaluate the impact of ad experiences. Read more

Amazon Updates Product Bundling Requirements for 2027

Amazon is updating its product bundling policy effective January 11, 2027. Most single-ASIN bundles must be packaged by the original manufacturer or brand, unless sellers have authorization to repackage them. Noncompliant bundles may be deactivated and flagged as Account Health violations, so sellers should review their listings before the deadline. Read more

Walmart Introduces Bulk Attribute Editor for Listing Quality

Walmart has introduced Bulk Attribute Editor, allowing sellers to update multiple listing attributes, such as titles, descriptions, key features, and images, at once. The tool helps improve Listing Quality scores by identifying and fixing attribute issues across multiple products more efficiently. Read more

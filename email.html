Amazon’s AI Shopping Tool Draws Pushback From Online Retailers

Amazon is facing backlash from independent retailers over its experimental Shop Direct and Buy for Me AI shopping features, which list and purchase products from other brands’ websites sometimes without consent. Several merchants say their products were scraped, mislisted, or sold despite not opting in, raising concerns around control, inventory accuracy, and forced participation. The controversy highlights growing tension as Amazon and other tech giants push deeper into agentic AI commerce, reshaping how online shopping works and who controls the customer relationship. Walmart Begins Testing Ads Inside AI Shopping Assistant Sparky

Walmart is testing sponsored ads within Sparky, its AI shopping assistant in the Walmart app, allowing brands to appear in product recommendation conversations. The move positions Sparky as a new advertising surface competing with Amazon’s Rufus and builds on Walmart’s broader AI strategy, including advertiser tools like Marty for campaign optimization and AI-generated creative. With massive shopper scale and growing AI-driven ad tools, Walmart is signaling that AI-powered commerce and advertising will be a major growth channel in 2026. Read more

Google Shopping Expands Promotion Support Starting January 2026

Beginning January 2026, Google Merchant Center will support more promotion types on Google Shopping. Merchants can now run discounts on subscription fees, including free trials and “subscribe and save” offers, use common promo abbreviations like BOGO, B1G1, MRP, and MSRP, and in Brazil only offer promotions tied to specific payment methods such as digital wallet cashback. These updates make it easier for merchants to create clearer, more flexible promotions across Google surfaces. Read more

Shopify Brings Shopping Directly Into AI Conversations

Shopify is making it easier for brands to sell wherever AI conversations happen. With its new agentic commerce platform and the Universal Commerce Protocol (built with Google), merchants can now let customers discover and buy products directly inside AI engines like Google AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and ChatGPT.
